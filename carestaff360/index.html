FAQ

Questions agencies ask.

Is it HIPAA-ready?

It is built with HIPAA safeguards: role-based access, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logs of every view and change, and daily backups. Patient details stay inside the platform instead of personal texts.

Do caregivers need to install an app?

No. It runs in any phone or computer browser. Caregivers get alerts and claim shifts from their phone.

Can you move our existing staff data in?

Yes. Setup includes importing your staff list and documents, and turning your forms into fillable templates.

Who owns the data?

You do. On a one-time project, the code, database and domain sit in your own accounts.

Can it be customised?

Yes. The Custom package adds features such as a CRM, extra reports or a mobile app, quoted to your needs.

How long does setup take?

About 1–2 weeks for most agencies, from demo to go-live.
