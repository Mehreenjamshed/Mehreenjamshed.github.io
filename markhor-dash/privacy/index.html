Markhor Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Markhor Dash is a running game about Pakistan for players of all ages, including children. The game does not ask for names, emails, photos, contacts or location, has no accounts or chat, and does not sell any data.

Information the game keeps

Your progress (levels finished, mangoes collected, runners unlocked, daily missions and sound setting) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Optional ads

Ads never play on their own. A player may choose to watch a short video ad to get one more life or to double the mangoes from a level. If the player taps "No thanks", no ad is shown.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. We set every ad request as directed to children and set the content rating to "General", so ads are not personalised and are limited to content suitable for all ages. To show an ad, AdMob may process information such as the device's app set ID, IP address, device type and app activity, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, as described in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Players in the UK and Europe

In the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game uses Google's consent message to ask for permission before ad data is processed, as privacy law there requires. Players can choose "Consent", "Do not consent" or "Manage options". Choosing "Do not consent" does not stop anyone from playing; the game works the same either way.

Children

Markhor Dash is made for families and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no purchases, no links to social media and no way to talk to other players.

Permissions

The game uses the internet only to load optional ads. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please write to the developer email address shown on the Markhor Dash page in the Google Play Store.
